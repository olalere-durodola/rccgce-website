Welcome.

Welcome to RCCG Covenant Embassy, a family-friendly, fast-growing RCCG parish located in the heart of Plano, Dallas. We'd love to have you and your family join us for a worship experience like no other — power-packed, spirit-filled, and rooted in genuine community.

As a Redeemed Christian Church of God parish, our mission is simple: to see souls saved, lives transformed, and families strengthened through the uncompromised Word of God. Every service is an opportunity to encounter God's presence in an atmosphere of warmth, holiness, and love.

You'll also receive sound, practical, and anointed teaching from our Pastor, Pastor Shola Adeyemo, whose ministry is grounded in Scripture and committed to helping you grow in faith and purpose.

Come as you are — we can't wait to welcome you home.
