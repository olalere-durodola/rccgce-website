Website privacy.

How this public review version handles viewing, contact links, and external services. Updated October 18, 2026.

This public review version

This review website is publicly viewable on GitHub Pages. The current church website remains at www.rccgcovenantembassy.org. GitHub processes request information, including visitor IP addresses, to host and protect this website. This version has no account sign-in. GitHub Privacy Statement.

Video and YouTube

The homepage preview and service player may load YouTube and attempt muted playback when the media enters view. Automatic playback is suppressed for reduced-motion and save-data preferences where supported. You can pause, close, or manually play a video.

Viewing connects to YouTube, which may process viewing and technical information under Google’s privacy terms. The YouTube IFrame API is used to control playback and recover from errors. Privacy-enhanced embedding does not make viewing anonymous.

Google privacy information

Contact and prayer

This redesign has no visitor submission form or visitor database. Email links open your email app; you decide what to include and whether to send. Avoid sharing sensitive personal or medical details through public chats or general contact messages.

Calendars and external services

Calendar files are generated locally in your browser and downloaded to your device. They do not register attendance or send your details to the church. Directions open Google Maps; Bible Study opens Zoom; member discussions and giving checkout open the church’s existing website. Their providers’ own practices apply.

Analytics and storage

The redesign includes no analytics provider, advertising tracker, or client-side cookies/storage of its own. Embedded providers and hosting may process information separately. A church-approved privacy policy and migration review are still needed before launch on the church’s own domain.

Questions

Email covenantembassy@gmail.com. This notice describes this preview’s implementation; it does not establish the church’s wider retention or legal policies.
