About the event

Just Worship 2026 | One Heart. One Sound. One King.

What if, for three hours, there was no agenda other than worshiping God?

No long program. No distractions. No denominational barriers. Just voices lifted, hearts surrendered, and a room full of people who came for one reason: to spend time in the presence of God.

Just Worship 2026 is three hours of uninterrupted worship through songs, praise, and heartfelt moments before God.

If you love to worship, if you have been longing for time simply set apart for God, or if you just need a place to come as you are and turn your attention back to Him, this is your invitation.

Come alone. Bring your family. Invite your friends. Bring someone who needs to be in the room.
