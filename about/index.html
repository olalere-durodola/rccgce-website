Who We Are.

RCCG Covenant Embassy is a Redeemed Christian Church of God parish in Plano, Texas.

Worship With Us

A place of welcome.

We are a church that seeks to be a place of welcome, hope, and transformation. We strive to be a place of belonging where everyone is accepted and loved. We are a church that seeks to be a place of worship, prayer, and service to our community. Our church aims to be a place of learning and growth, where we can grow in our faith and understanding of God's Word. Our church seeks to be a place of mission where we can share the good news of Jesus Christ with our neighbors.

As a church, we are a community of individuals united by our faith in God and our desire to serve and honor Him. We are committed to living according to the teachings of the Bible and striving to follow in Christ's footsteps by loving our neighbors as ourselves, serving those in need, and sharing the gospel's good news. Our church is where people from all walks of life are welcome, regardless of age, ethnicity, social status, background, past experiences, or current struggles. We believe that working together as a community can better serve God's purposes and build His kingdom on earth.

Our Beliefs

We believe in the Divine inspiration of the Bible and the infallible Word of God. The Divine Trinity consists of three Persons: The Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost. The Deity of Jesus Christ, in His virgin birth, sinless life, teachings, crucifixion, resurrection, ascension, and coming return to this earth in power and glory to rule.

God created man in the image of God before whom he walked in innocence, holiness, and purity, but by voluntary disobedience and transgression, he fell to the depths of sin. Therefore, the only means of being cleansed from sin is through repentance and faith in the finished work at Calvary.

Perfection is a spiritual growth for those sanctified and consecrated unto God. The Baptism of the Holy Ghost for all believers living blessed lives is evidenced by speaking in tongues as the Spirit gives utterances. That Divine Healing of sickness for all believers is in the atonement.

Our Vision

At RCCG Covenant Embassy, Our Vision is to build a “Family Church” where those who are hurting, depressed, frustrated, and confused can find love, acceptance, help, guidance, and encouragement. We pursue the Glory of God through worship, ministry, giving, and education. We strive to connect with Jesus Christ and to each other, in order to magnify the name of the Lord our God. At the heart of everything we do is the understanding that we must love God with all our hearts, with all our souls, with all our minds, and we must also treat our neighbors as we wish to be treated ourselves.

Our Community

Our mission is to Plant and cultivate God’s Garden, build God’s building, harvest God’s field & grow Christ’s body.. RCCG Covenant Embassy has a long, proud history of serving the community of Plano, Tx. Through the dedication of our leadership, pastors, and volunteers, we are committed to the teachings of Jesus Christ and are here to spread His message. We are open to worshippers of all ages and backgrounds and strive to lead all who worship with us toward a deeper love for God.
