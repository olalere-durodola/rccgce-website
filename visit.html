When should I arrive?

Worship begins at 11 am Central. Prayer and Sunday School begin from 10 am. Contact the church about holiday or special-service changes.

Where do you meet?

3900 Pebblecreek Court, Suite 102, Plano, TX 75023. Get directions

Can I bring my family?

Yes. Covenant Embassy describes itself as a family church and welcomes people from different backgrounds. Contact the church for any specific arrangements you need.

Can I join online?

Yes. Visit Watch for recorded services and the official live-channel link. The church streams on YouTube during service when a broadcast is available.

How can I request prayer?

Contact the church by email. The link opens your email app so you can choose what to share and send.
